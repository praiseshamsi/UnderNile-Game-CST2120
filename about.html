About UnderNile

The Story

Deep beneath the Nile, a young girl searches for a way home. She must travel through a hidden underwater world.

The Mission

Run through each level, avoid rocks, and stay safe for as long as you can.

How to Play

  • Press Up Arrow or Space to jump.
  • Hold Down Arrow to slide.
  • Avoid obstacles and protect your health.
  • Complete levels and earn points.
